# Glossary — Measuring the User Experience (Tullis & Albert, 1st ed.)

**A/B test** — live-site comparison of alternative page versions shown simultaneously to different visitors (Ch 9)
**Accuracy** — errors transformed so higher = better: 1 − errors ÷ max errors observed (Ch 8)
**Adjusted Wald** — recommended method for binomial confidence intervals on small-sample task success (Ch 4)
**After-Scenario Questionnaire (ASQ)** — Lewis's 3 post-scenario items: satisfaction with ease, time, support info (Ch 6)
**Assistance** — moderator reset, restating/probing, answering, or outside help; downgrades success level (Ch 4)
**Awareness–usefulness gap** — % aware of a feature vs. % rating it useful (top-2-box); large gap → promote it (Ch 6)
**Baseline** — first measurement that later iterations are compared to (Ch 1, 11)
**Between-subjects** — each participant sees one condition; no carryover, larger n needed (Ch 2)
**Binary success** — pass/fail coded 1/0; mean = success rate (Ch 4)
**Carryover effect** — practice or fatigue from one condition affecting the next (Ch 2)
**Chi-square test** — compares observed vs. expected category counts; use raw counts (Ch 2, 9)
**Click-through rate** — clicks ÷ impressions of a link (Ch 9)
**Confidence interval** — range likely containing the true population value; always report (Ch 2, 11)
**Counterbalancing** — varying task/condition order to cancel order effects (Ch 2)
**CSUQ / PSSUQ** — Lewis's 19-item post-study questionnaires; 4 factors (Ch 6, 10)
**CUE studies** — Molich's comparative evaluations showing low agreement among teams (Ch 5)
**Drop-off rate** — share of users abandoning at each step of a flow (Ch 9)
**Efficiency** — effort to complete a task: actions/steps, or success rate ÷ time (Ch 4)
**Error** — incorrect action that may lead to task failure; outcome of an issue (Ch 4)
**Error opportunity** — a specific chance to make a mistake (Ch 4)
**Evaluator effect** — raters judge issues they found as more severe (Ch 5)
**Expectation measure** — expected vs. experienced difficulty per task; quadrant analysis (Ch 6)
**Expert/actual ratio** — expert time ÷ participant time; overall usability score (Ch 8)
**FirstClick testing** — scoring only the first click after reading a scenario (Ch 10)
**Fixation** — pause of the eye in one area; basis of eye-tracking metrics (Ch 7)
**Formative study** — iterative testing to improve a design before it's final (Ch 3)
**Geometric mean** — mean of logs; suits skewed time data (Ch 2, 4)
**Harvey Balls** — five-level filled-circle icons for scorecards (Ch 8)
**Interval data** — equal intervals, no true zero (SUS, Likert) (Ch 2)
**Issue (usability)** — underlying cause of a problem: blocks, derails, confuses, causes errors (Ch 5)
**Learnability** — change in an efficiency metric across trials (Ch 4)
**Levels of success** — partial-credit success scoring (Ch 4)
**Likert scale** — agreement with a statement on an odd-point scale (Ch 6)
**Lostness** — sqrt[(N/S−1)² + (R/N−1)²]; >0.5 = lost (Ch 4)
**Magic number 5** — claim that ~5 users find ~80% of issues (p≈0.3) (Ch 5)
**Nominal data** — unordered categories (binary success) (Ch 2)
**Ordinal data** — ordered categories without equal intervals (severity) (Ch 2)
**p (probability of detection)** — share of issues one participant reveals; found = 1−(1−p)ⁿ (Ch 5)
**Positive finding** — something that worked well; report it first (Ch 5, 11)
**Product Reaction Cards** — Microsoft's 118 adjectives for qualitative reactions (Ch 6)
**Ratio data** — interval data with a true zero (time) (Ch 2)
**Semantic differential** — bipolar adjective rating scale (Ch 6)
**Severity rating** — priority of an issue by UX impact (± frequency, business impact) (Ch 5)
**Social desirability bias** — inflated ratings when observed (Ch 6)
**Stopping rule** — when to end an unsuccessful task (Ch 4)
**Summative study** — evaluation against goals/competitors after design (Ch 3)
**SUM (Single Usability Metric)** — Sauro & Kindlund's standardized blend of completion, time, errors, satisfaction (Ch 8)
**SUS (System Usability Scale)** — Brooke's 10-item, 0–100 post-session score (Ch 6)
**Time-on-task** — elapsed time from task start to end (Ch 4)
**Top-2-box** — % choosing the two highest scale points (Ch 6)
**Usability** — extent to which specified users achieve specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction (ISO 9241-11) (Ch 1)
**Usability scorecard** — chart summarizing several metrics, often per task (Ch 8)
**Within-subjects** — same participants try all conditions (Ch 2)
**z-score** — (x − mean) ÷ SD; equal-weight combination for comparing subsets (Ch 8)
